Actuators & microrobots

Actuation for sensing

Microrobots are commonly designed for delivery and surgery. We expand their role to sensing, with miniature robotic systems that probe biological processes and measure physical properties.

Remote control

Remotely controlled microrobots

Advanced manufacturing approaches enable parallel fabrication of compact 3D microstructures across diverse materials. External fields drive bending, twisting and locomotion for remote control of deformation and movement at small scales.

RELATED PUBLICATIONSScience Robotics 2022, 7, eabn0602.Advanced Materials 2021, 33, 2102691.

Active sensing

Magnetic implants for wireless biosensing

Magnetic pulses excite miniature implants whose oscillations reveal fluid viscosity, pressure and molecular binding. A wearable transceiver provides wireless excitation and readout to monitor local conditions within the body.

RELATED PUBLICATIONSScience Advances 2024, 10, eadm9314.
