Wearables & e-skins

Sensing at the interface

Flexible sensors enable conformal integration with diverse surfaces. We integrate them onto the human body, robots, and instruments to digitize movement, touch, and physical interactions.

Magnetic assembly

Reconfigurable wearable devices

Magnetic interfaces enable reversible assembly and reconfiguration of soft electronic modules. These wearable systems can integrate with AI for biomechanical monitoring and have been used in clinical studies.

RELATED PUBLICATIONSAdvanced Materials 2024, 36, 2308575.Advanced Healthcare Materials 2026, 15, e05765.

Magnetic tactile sensing

Multimodal tactile sensing through magnetic fields

Stacked sensors and magnetic elastomers resolve complex mechanical stimuli. A wireless glove integrates these capabilities to quantify tissue interactions during clinical examinations and procedures.

RELATED PUBLICATIONSNature Communications 2026, accepted.Small 2026, 22, e74405.

3D e-skins

3D micro strain gauges for scalable e-skins

MEMS-compatible fabrication creates high-density, large-area flexible arrays of 3D micro strain gauges. These arrays distinguish normal and shear forces for tactile sensing on robots and medical instruments.

RELATED PUBLICATIONSScience Advances 2024, 10, eadp6094.Science Translational Medicine 2020, 12, eabc4327.Nature Biomedical Engineering 2020, 4, 997-1009.Nature Electronics 2019, 2, 26-35.
