Overview

Sensors reveal how living systems function and how we interact with the physical world. We develop advanced sensors through innovations in materials and structural design to bridge the biological, physical, and digital worlds, with goals in understanding living systems and improving human health. Our work takes the form of wireless implants, actuators & microrobotics, and wearable devices & e-skins.
